Privacy Policy for Q-Inspect

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Q-Inspect, an enterprise quality inspection and management application operated by Energxiq ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when employees, contractors, and authorized partners access and use the Q-Inspect mobile application.

Note: Q-Inspect is an enterprise-grade internal quality control tool. Access is strictly restricted to authorized personnel with company-issued credentials.

2. Information We Collect

To facilitate quality inspections, tracker reporting, and progress auditing across projects, the application collects:

  • Account Information: Corporate email address, authentication credentials, user role, and assigned project/business unit.
  • Inspection & Field Data: Quality check reports, NCR (Non-Conformance Reports), punch points, checklist entries, training logs, and equipment quality records submitted by you.
  • Camera & Photo Media: Images or videos captured or uploaded by the user directly within the app as evidence for quality inspections, site audits, and material defect logging.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, network status, and crash analytics logs (processed securely via Google Firebase Crashlytics) to troubleshoot crashes and improve app stability.

3. Permissions Requested & How They Are Used

Q-Inspect requests only necessary device permissions for essential app functionality:

  • Camera Permission (CAMERA): Used exclusively to allow users to take real-time photos/videos of solar site equipment, installations, and punch point defects during inspections.
  • Storage / Media Permission (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used to allow users to attach existing site photos or reference documents (e.g., drawings, inspection checklists, PDF reports) to quality tickets.
  • Internet (INTERNET): Used to securely synchronize inspection records with enterprise backend servers and authenticate user sessions.

4. How We Use Collected Data

All data collected is used solely for legitimate business operations:

  • Authenticating authorized users and enforcing role-based permissions.
  • Recording and tracking quality audits, punch points, and site installations.
  • Generating project inspection reports for operational quality compliance.
  • Diagnosing application crashes and maintaining technical reliability.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user or enterprise data to third-party advertisers.

5. Third-Party Services

The application integrates with select enterprise service providers solely for technical app maintenance:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: Used to monitor application performance, detect system crashes, and identify bugs. Firebase processes crash logs and anonymous device metadata in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for data transmitted between the mobile application and our servers. Data is retained in accordance with Energxiq's corporate data retention policies for operational and compliance record-keeping.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play's user data policies, users have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data and account records:

  • Account Deletion: Authorized personnel can request complete account deactivation and deletion of personal profile data by emailing support@energxiq.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Q-Inspect".
  • Retention Scope: Personal login identifiers and profile associations will be deleted upon verified request. However, operational quality inspection logs, punch lists, and project compliance records previously submitted may be retained in anonymized or archived form strictly for audit, legal, and safety regulatory compliance.

8. Children's Privacy

Q-Inspect is an enterprise, commercial quality management application strictly intended for use by adult business personnel, contractors, and employees. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 18 in relevant jurisdictions). If we discover that personal data from a child has inadvertently been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our servers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect modifications in our operational practices, regulatory guidelines, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly to this URL with an updated "Last modified" date.
